\subsection{Dataset}
\label{sec:dataset}

\textsc{SciFig-Eval} comprises 250 English-language scientific figures extracted from arXiv papers, spanning bar charts (99), line plots (99), and pie charts (52). Each figure is paired with a structured expert annotation describing axes, data series, labels, color mappings, and trends, verified against the original PDF context. We additionally record source page numbers and verified figure numbers, enabling in-paper evaluation where the model receives the full PDF page.

\paragraph{Evaluation subsets.}
From the 250-figure pool, we construct a primary subset of 100 figures (40 bar, 40 line, 20 pie) via stratified random sampling (seed$=$42), and a nested ablation subset of 50 figures (20/20/10) for probe-designer independence studies (\S\ref{sec:ablation}).

\paragraph{Adversarial stimuli.}
We construct four categories targeting distinct behavioral dimensions: \emph{resistance probes} (250 figures, three types per figure grounded in cognitive science), \emph{caption bias probes} (100 figures with modified captions embedding 2--3 false claims), \emph{admittance blur} (50 figures with unrecoverable elements selectively blurred), and \emph{inductance blur} (48 figures with context-inferable elements blurred). Selective blur candidates were identified through multi-model consensus and confirmed via human review on a purpose-built dashboard. Full construction details appear in Appendix~\ref{sec:dataset-details}.
