# P0-1 · A-R-I Framing

Working document. Two reviewers raised this. This is the single most consequential item because it touches how the paper positions its central contribution.

## What the reviewers actually said

**No6d** (novelty critique):
> "The A-R-I framework largely renames and reorganizes known behavioral dimensions, and it is unclear whether it amounts to a substantially new evaluation method."
>
> Suggestion: "position A-R-I more modestly, as a diagnostic taxonomy rather than a new evaluation paradigm."

**LhRb** (sufficiency critique):
> "The A-R-I framework is useful, but I am not fully convinced that these three dimensions are sufficient to cover behavioral reliability."
>
> Suggestion: "The A-R-I dimensions could be better justified as a necessary and relatively complete decomposition."

These are two distinct critiques and they need distinct responses.

---

## Diagnosing what's really being said

### No6d's critique — novelty
No6d is claiming A-R-I is a repackaging. This is *partially* true and we should not pretend otherwise:
- Uncertainty acknowledgment (Admittance) has been studied under labels like calibration, refusal, "I don't know" behaviour (Kadavath 2022, Yin 2023).
- Resistance to misleading input has been studied as sycophancy (Perez 2022, Sharma 2023) and prompt-injection robustness.
- Contextual inference from partial evidence has been touched on in ChartQA-style reasoning tests and hallucination benchmarks (POPE, MMHal-Bench, HallusionBench).

**What is genuinely new that we can defend:**
1. **Joint operationalisation on one corpus.** No prior VLM figure benchmark runs all three probe families on the same figures with the same models. The value is not in inventing new probe types — it is in the **empirical dissociation** we demonstrate.
2. **Selective-blur probes with OCR-anchored coordinate mapping + dual-stage grey-blend + Gaussian.** This is a genuinely novel probe *design* (h9tb praised it explicitly as "exceptionally precise data interventions"). We have not seen an equivalent in prior work.
3. **The A/I separation via unrecoverable vs inferable blur targets.** This lets us distinguish "admits blur" from "over-refuses when context is sufficient" — a discrimination no prior benchmark makes.
4. **The empirical rank inversions** (GPT-5.2 highest MQM but lowest resistance; Gemini highest admittance but lower inductance). These prove the axes are not redundant.

### LhRb's critique — sufficiency
LhRb is asking a different question: even granting the taxonomy, why exactly *these* three? Why not calibration? Why not faithfulness under paraphrase? Why not consistency across languages?

This one is fair and we haven't argued it carefully in the paper. We name three axes and go. We do not argue their **necessity** (each captures a risk the others miss) or their **completeness relative to a well-defined scope** (which behavioural risks we claim to cover and which we don't).

## Strategic options

### Option A — Full concession
Rewrite everywhere to say "diagnostic taxonomy," drop novelty claims for A-R-I entirely, lean on the corpus + probe pipeline as the paper's contribution.
- **Pro:** Directly satisfies No6d's framing suggestion.
- **Con:** Neuters the paper's central conceptual contribution. Excitement scores probably stay flat. LhRb's sufficiency concern is untouched.

### Option B — Full defence
Argue A-R-I *is* a novel framework because the empirical dissociation is new, and the probe design is new.
- **Pro:** Keeps the framing intact.
- **Con:** Ignores what No6d literally asked for. Reviewer gets more entrenched.

### Option C — Hybrid: concede vocabulary, defend substance, add scoping argument *(recommended)*
- Reframe "framework" → "diagnostic decomposition / behavioural taxonomy" in the paper's language.
- Add a paragraph explicitly citing prior work each axis extends.
- Add a **necessity + sufficiency-within-scope** argument (see draft below).
- Lead the rebuttal with a concession, then the substance.

Option C is the strong move because it hands No6d the vocabulary win, hands LhRb the scoping argument, and preserves everything the paper actually shows empirically.

## The necessity + sufficiency-within-scope argument

This is the missing paragraph that would satisfy LhRb and blunt No6d.

### Necessity — each axis captures a distinct deployment failure

- **Admittance failure** = silent fabrication when evidence is *missing*. Deployment analogue: a research assistant confidently reporting a numeric value from a redacted or low-resolution figure. Existing hallucination benchmarks (POPE, HallusionBench) largely test fabrication when evidence is *present but misread*; A specifically targets the *evidence-absent* case.

- **Resistance failure** = incorporating misleading context (false caption, false premise) into an answer. Deployment analogue: multimodal RAG pipelines where the retrieved caption is incorrect, or user queries built on wrong premises. Prior sycophancy work covers opinion-conforming; R covers factual-premise acceptance grounded in image vs text conflict.

- **Inductance failure** = refusing or fabricating when partial evidence *does* support a justified inference. Deployment analogue: a model that says "cannot determine" for a chart whose answer is fully constrained by unblurred axis labels + surrounding context. This is the **counterpart failure to Admittance**: a model that maximises admittance by refusing everything scores 1.0 on A but 0.0 on I.

**The key insight to sell in the rebuttal:** A and I are in tension. Neither alone is the target — you need both, and R is orthogonal to both. That tension is the argument for at least these three axes.

### Sufficiency-within-scope — what we claim, what we don't

We are **not** claiming A-R-I is complete for all VLM behaviour. We are claiming it captures the primary deployment risks *specific to scientific-figure understanding under uncertainty*. Other behavioural traits — calibration confidence intervals, cross-lingual consistency, refusal harm — matter but are out of scope for this benchmark. We offer A-R-I as a starting taxonomy that others can extend along additional axes as the scope broadens.

### Empirical distinctness — already in the paper, needs to be foregrounded

Table 9 (cross-dimensional correlations) already shows the three axes are only weakly correlated across models. The rank inversions (Gemini leads A, GPT-5.2 leads Ip, Claude leads R on some probes) are the empirical proof that no axis is redundant. **We should move this into the framework section, not leave it in the results.**

## Draft rebuttal response — for A-R-I framing (No6d + LhRb)

> We thank both reviewers for pressing on the framing of A-R-I. We agree that the individual axes build on existing behavioural work — uncertainty acknowledgment (Kadavath 2022, Yin 2023), sycophancy and premise-acceptance (Perez 2022, Sharma 2023), and hallucination under partial evidence (POPE, HallusionBench) — and we should have foregrounded that lineage more explicitly. In the camera-ready we will:
>
> (1) Reposition A-R-I as a **diagnostic decomposition / behavioural taxonomy** for figure-reading trustworthiness under uncertainty, rather than a new evaluation paradigm (No6d).
>
> (2) Add a paragraph in Section 3 mapping each axis to the prior behavioural axis it extends, and stating precisely what our joint operationalisation contributes: (a) all three probe families applied to the same corpus and models; (b) a novel selective-blur probe design with OCR-anchored coordinate mapping that admittance/inductance separation would not be possible without; (c) empirical dissociation between axes, evidenced by rank inversions across models and by low cross-dimensional correlations (Table 9).
>
> (3) Add an explicit **necessity and scope** argument (LhRb): each axis captures a deployment risk not captured by the others (silent fabrication under missing evidence, susceptibility to misleading text context, and failure of justified inference from partial evidence); Admittance and Inductance stand in tension — a model that refuses everything scores 1.0 on A but 0.0 on I, so both axes are required to characterise trustworthy behaviour. We do not claim A-R-I is complete for all VLM behavioural reliability; we scope it to figure-reading under uncertainty and invite extension.

That's roughly 220 words. Tight enough for a rebuttal.
