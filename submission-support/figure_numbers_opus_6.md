## fig_126
- figure_number: Figure 4
- caption: (LEFT) The trend of Pass@1 rate on Visual Navigation as the number of reasoning steps increases. We observe that LVLMs significantly benefit from longer reasoning chains, although the effect plateaus rapidly beyond a certain threshold of reasoning steps.
