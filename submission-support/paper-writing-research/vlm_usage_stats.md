## 1. Consumer User Numbers

## 2. Vision/Image-Specific Usage

### ChatGPT Image Generation (GPT-4o native images)
- **130 million users** generated **700+ million images** in the first week after GPT-4o image generation launched (March 25, 2025) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/03/chatgpt-users-have-generated-over-700m-images-since-last-week-openai-says/))
- That is ~1,200 images generated per second
- The feature caused millions of new signups and strained OpenAI infrastructure
- NOTE: This is image *generation*, not image *understanding/upload*. OpenAI has **not** publicly disclosed what percentage of conversations involve image uploads for understanding.

### Key gap
- No public data exists on the percentage of ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude users who specifically use vision/image upload features for image *understanding* (as opposed to generation). This is a notable gap in available statistics.

### Azure Computer Vision
- **2,704+ companies** use Azure Computer Vision API globally as of 2025 ([6sense](https://6sense.com/tech/api-management/azure-computer-vision-market-share))
- Top country: US (1,114 companies), India (278), UK (141)

### Google Cloud Vision API
- **131 companies** tracked as using Google Cloud Vision API ([Enlyft](https://enlyft.com/tech/products/google-cloud-vision-api))
- Primarily used by companies with >10,000 employees and >$1B revenue

### OpenAI API
- API access accounts for ~25% of OpenAI revenue ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/openai/))
- Vision endpoints available since November 2023 (GPT-4V API) and May 2024 (GPT-4o)
- No public breakdown of vision vs. text-only API calls

## 7. Research Community

### Publication Volume
- A survey of **26,104 accepted papers** from CVPR, ICLR, and NeurIPS (2023-2025) documents the rise of vision-language-LLM research ([arXiv:2510.09586](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09586))
- The survey identifies three macro shifts: (1) sharp rise of multimodal VLM work reframing perception as instruction-following; (2) generative methods consolidating around controllability and distillation; (3) 3D/video moving from NeRFs to Gaussian splatting

### Developer Community
- **84% of developers** use or plan to use AI tools (2025 Stack Overflow survey, 49,000+ respondents from 177 countries) ([Stack Overflow](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025))
- Up from 76% in 2024

## 9. Key Gaps & Caveats

- **No public data** on what % of LLM users specifically upload images for *understanding* (vs. generation). Companies treat this as proprietary.
- Anthropic's $30B annualized revenue figure (Apr 2026) is exceptionally high and should be verified against primary sources before citing.
- Enterprise "adoption" numbers (e.g., "92% of Fortune 500 use ChatGPT") may reflect any usage, not necessarily deep integration or multimodal usage.
- Market size estimates vary significantly by research firm ($1.6B to $2.7B for 2025 alone).
- The 26K papers survey covers top-3 venues only; total VLM-related publications across all venues and arXiv would be much higher.
