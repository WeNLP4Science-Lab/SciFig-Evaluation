## 5. 2026 Papers on VLM Evaluation and Failures

### Chart Hallucination

**ChartHal** (arxiv 2509.17481): Fine-grained hallucination evaluation framework for chart understanding.
- Dataset: 1,062 human-validated samples
- **GPT-5 accuracy: only 34.46%** on chart hallucination detection
- **o4-mini accuracy: only 22.79%**
- Questions involving absent or contradictory information especially trigger hallucinations
- Source: [arxiv.org/abs/2509.17481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17481)

**ChartNOISe / "Losing the Plot"** (arxiv 2509.18425): How VLM responses degrade on imperfect charts.
- Hallucination forms: value fabrication, trend misinterpretation, entity confusion, reasoning hallucination, table/translation drift
- Source: [arxiv.org/pdf/2509.18425](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.18425)

### Perception Bottleneck

**"On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding"** (arxiv 2503.18435, EMNLP 2025 Findings):
- Vision encoder is the limiting factor in chart understanding
- Visual representations contain richer information than linear extractors capture
- Instruction tuning improves extraction but cannot fix vision encoder limitations
- Source: [arxiv.org/abs/2503.18435](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18435)

### VLM Hallucination Detection (2026)

**HALP** (arxiv 2603.05465, March 2026): Detecting hallucinations in VLMs without generating a single token.
- Source: [arxiv.org/abs/2603.05465](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05465)

**Benchmarking Deflection and Hallucination in LVLMs** (arxiv 2604.12033, April 2026):
- Source: [arxiv.org/abs/2604.12033](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12033)

### VLM Robustness (2026)

**VLM-RobustBench** (arxiv 2603.06148, March 2026): Comprehensive robustness benchmark for VLMs.
- Source: [arxiv.org/html/2603.06148](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.06148)

### Stanford AI Index 2026 -- "Jagged Frontier" Finding

- Frontier models achieve PhD-level scores on science benchmarks and IMO gold-medal-level math
- Yet read analog clocks correctly **only 50.1% of the time**
- GPT-4o and Claude 3.5 Sonnet scored **below 50%** on analog clock reading
- SWE-bench Verified: performance rose from 60% to near 100% in one year
- Humanity's Last Exam: best models went from 8.8% to >50% accuracy (as of April 2026)
- "Headline benchmark scores are a poor proxy for performance on the specific task your system needs to complete"
- Source: [Stanford HAI AI Index 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report), [R&D World](https://www.rdworldonline.com/stanford-hai-2026-ai-index-ai-posts-gains-in-science-and-medicine-while-often-struggling-to-read-a-clock/)

### Chart-Specific Research (2026)

**Chart-CoCa** (arxiv 2508.11975): Self-improving chart understanding via code-driven synthesis.
- Source: [arxiv.org/html/2508.11975](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.11975)

**ChartScope** (ICLR 2026): LVLM optimized for chart comprehension with Dual-Path training strategy.
- Source: [openreview.net](https://openreview.net/pdf/bdec5841ec60801a5389e756480cfaf331418703.pdf)
