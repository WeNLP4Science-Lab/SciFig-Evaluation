Conditions are grouped into perceptual transforms (original without caption, noise, low contrast, rotation), page-context embedding (in-paper), and adversarial conditions (caption bias, admittance blur, inductance blur). Rotation causes the largest perceptual drop across all models ($\sim$20 points). Caption bias scores remain near or above baseline, indicating that the MQM judge does not reliably detect false claims embedded in modified captions. Admittance blur (unrecoverable elements) degrades quality more than inductance blur (inferable elements), consistent with the A-R-I distinction.}
