  \caption{A-R-I behavioural profiles across 8 models. GPT-5.2 (blue), Gemini (green), and Phi-4 (red) are highlighted. \textbf{(a)}~Admittance: percentage of probes where the model acknowledged visual uncertainty. Circles~=~active (targeted question), squares~=~passive (open-ended description), lines connect paired measurements per model. Gemini admits uncertainty 90\% of the time (active) and 74\% (passive); no other model exceeds 26\%. The active-passive gap reveals that models express more uncertainty in open-ended descriptions than when directly questioned. \textbf{(b)}~Resistance: score range across three probe types ($\triangledown$~inexist, $\diamond$~contra, $\triangle$~unanswerable). Longer bars indicate greater vulnerability variation across deception techniques. Unanswerable probes are easiest to resist; inexist probes (presupposition embedding) are hardest. \textbf{(c)}~Inductance: percentage of fabricated answers that were correct for context-inferable elements. GPT-5.2 and Gemini achieve 68--80\% correctness, confirming genuine contextual inference rather than random fabrication.}
